## 3. Explicit Boundary: What Lies Outside Lean

We strictly separate machine-checked theorems from explicit cryptographic, empirical, and paper-level obligations (full dependency table in [`specs/07-assumptions-and-lean.md`](../specs/07-assumptions-and-lean.md)):

1. **Cryptographic & Polynomial-Commitment Premises (`A-ROM`, `A-AROM`, `A-PCS`, `A-PRG`)**: `H_out` (BLAKE3/SHA-3) as a classical programmable random oracle; Poseidon2 width-12 permutation as an ideal invertible permutation with `A-AROM` straight-line extraction consistency for in-circuit sponges; Basefold round-by-round unique-decoding proximity soundness (`A-PCS`, with query escape arithmetic verified in `basefold_qry_esc_le`); and AES-256-CTR pseudorandomness (`A-PRG`) for mask regeneration in the two-pass setup proof.
2. **Constraint-Compiler Instantiation (`CompiledCS`)**: `ProtocolBridge.lean` machine-checks the reduction from `ProtocolRBRSpec` (`P-ext`, `P-compile`, `P-loc`, `P-final`) to `RoundByRound.rbr_default_lifetime` and verifies zero-slack boundary-pair compilation (`BucketSampling.boundary_pair_sound_complete`); mechanical extraction of concrete circuit-builder code from Lean is outside scope.
3. **Kernel Rounding Contract (`H-contract`) & Floating-Point Propagation**: `H-contract` states that deployed GPU/accelerator kernels obey the per-operator rounding contract committed in `model_id` (affecting completeness only, never soundness). Real-valued floating-point error propagation formulas ([`specs/06-tolerance-and-cheats.md`](../specs/06-tolerance-and-cheats.md) §2.2) are proved on paper and empirically validated with zero honest violations across 392,928 blocks on `Qwen3-0.6B` and `Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct` ([`benchmarks/certified/`](../benchmarks/certified/README.md)); the recurrent floating-point scan template $\mathsf{Scan}$ for linear attention remains open.
4. **Multi-Proof Simulator & ROM Programming Book-Keeping**: `Mask.lean` and `MaskJoint.lean` (including `multiround_independent_causal` and `libra_mask_bijOn_hyperplane`) machine-check single-proof joint masking and causal multi-round transcript uniformity; multi-proof hybrid induction, ROM programming collision bounds ($q_H N_{\rm prog}/2^{256}$), and keyed-sponge PRF bounds are proved on paper in [`specs/03-zero-knowledge.md`](../specs/03-zero-knowledge.md).
